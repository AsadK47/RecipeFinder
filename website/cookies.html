Cookie Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2025

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help us provide a better user experience and understand how you use our site.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Understand how you use our website
  • Improve website performance
  • Analyze traffic and usage patterns

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Type Purpose Duration
Essential Required for basic site functionality Session
Analytics Google Analytics - understand usage 2 years
Preferences Remember your cookie consent choice 1 year

4. Third-Party Cookies

We use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to help us analyze website traffic. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies
  • Opt-Out Links:
    • Google Analytics: Opt-out
  • Our Cookie Banner: You can change your consent anytime

6. Browser Controls

Manage cookies in your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

7. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable cookies:

  • Some features may not work properly
  • We won't be able to remember your preferences
  • Analytics will not track your visit

We may update this Cookie Policy. Check this page periodically for changes.
