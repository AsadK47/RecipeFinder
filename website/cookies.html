Cookie Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2025

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to remember your theme preference (light/dark mode) and your cookie consent choice. That's it.

3. Types of Cookies

Type Purpose Duration
Essential Required for basic site functionality Session
Preferences Remember your theme and consent choices 1 year

4. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality.
