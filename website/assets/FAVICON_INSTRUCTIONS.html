🎨 Generate Proper Favicons

To create proper favicon files from your app icon:

Option 1: Online Tool (Easiest)

  1. Go to RealFaviconGenerator.net
  2. Upload your app icon (from RecipeFinder/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/)
  3. Download the generated package
  4. Copy all files to Website/assets/

Option 2: Manual Export

  1. Export your app icon as PNG (1024x1024)
  2. Use ImageMagick or Photoshop to resize:
    • favicon-16x16.png (16x16)
    • favicon-32x32.png (32x32)
    • apple-touch-icon.png (180x180)
    • og-image.png (1200x630 for social media)
