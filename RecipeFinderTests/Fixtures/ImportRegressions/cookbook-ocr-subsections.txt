STICKY SESAME
CHICKEN
SERVES 4
FOR THE CHICKEN
500g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
3 tbsp cornflour
1 egg, beaten
vegetable oil, for frying
FOR THE SAUCE
3 tbsp honey
2 tbsp soy sauce
1 tbsp rice vinegar
2 garlic cloves, crushed
1 tsp sesame seeds
1. Toss the chicken in the egg, then in the cornflour
until evenly coated.
2. Heat 2cm of oil in a wok and fry the chicken in batches
for 5-6 minutes until crisp. Drain on kitchen paper.
3. Mix the honey, soy sauce, vinegar and garlic in a small
pan and bubble for 2 minutes until sticky.
4. Toss the chicken through the sauce and scatter with
sesame seeds.
