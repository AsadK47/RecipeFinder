Viral feta pasta but make it 10x easier
You'll need:
- 200g block of feta
- 2 punnets cherry tomatoes (about 500g)
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
- 300g penne
- fresh basil
Steps:
- Put the tomatoes in a dish with the oil and chilli, feta in the middle.
- Bake at 200C for 30 mins.
- Meanwhile boil the pasta.
- Smash the feta and tomatoes together, stir in the pasta and basil.
