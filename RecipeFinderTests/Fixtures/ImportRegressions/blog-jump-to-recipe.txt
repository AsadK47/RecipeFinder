Ingredients

1x2x3x
▢ 4 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each)
▢ 1 tbsp. olive oil
▢ 4 tbsp. unsalted butter
▢ 4 cloves garlic, minced
▢ 1-1/2 teaspoons lemon zest
▢ 2 tablespoons lemon juice
▢ 1/2 cup chicken stock
▢ Salt and pepper, to taste
▢ Fresh parsley, chopped
Instructions
Pat the salmon dry and season both sides with salt and pepper.
Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the salmon skin side up for 4 minutes, then flip.
Advertisement
Add the butter, garlic and lemon zest and cook for 1 minute, spooning the butter over the fish.
Pour in the stock and lemon juice and simmer for 3 minutes until the salmon is cooked through.
Sprinkle with parsley and serve straight away.
Notes
Swap the stock for white wine if you like.
Leftovers keep in the fridge for 2 days.
