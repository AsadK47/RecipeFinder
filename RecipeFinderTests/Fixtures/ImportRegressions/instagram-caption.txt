15 minute creamy garlic pasta 🍝
Save this for later 📌 and share with someone who needs a quick dinner!
INGREDIENTS 👇
🍝 250g spaghetti
🧄 4 garlic cloves, grated
🧈 2 tbsp butter
🥛 200ml double cream
🧀 50g parmesan, finely grated
🌿 a handful of basil
Method:
1️⃣ Cook the pasta in salted water until al dente. Save a mug of the cooking water.
2️⃣ Melt the butter in a pan and fry the garlic for 1 minute.
3️⃣ Pour in the cream, bring to a simmer and stir in the parmesan.
4️⃣ Toss the pasta through the sauce, loosening with pasta water, and finish with basil.
