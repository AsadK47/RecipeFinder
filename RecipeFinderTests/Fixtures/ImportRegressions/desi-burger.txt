Desi burger
https://www.thespruceeats.com/indian-style-burger-1957599
Prep:15 mins
Cook:16 mins
Total:31 mins
Servings: 6.0 203.3333333
Ingredients
750 500 grams (1 pound) ground beef or lamb
1.5 1 teaspoon ginger paste
3 2 teaspoons garlic paste
1.5 1 small bunch cilantro, finely chopped
3 2 medium green chilies, finely chopped
3 2 slices bread, soaked in water until soft and then torn
1.5 1 teaspoon ground coriander
0.75 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
1.5 1 teaspoon garam masala powder
0.75 1/2 teaspoon fine salt, more to taste
1.5 1 teaspoon freshly squeezed lime juice
1 Egg to bind
Vegetable, sunflower or canola oil to shallow fry the burger patties
6 4 burger buns
1.5 1 tablespoon unsalted butter, for the buns
0.5 1/3 cup mint-coriander chutney, yogurt, or desired topping
3 2 large tomatoes, sliced crosswise
1.5 1 large onion, sliced into rings
Steps to Make It
In a large bowl, place the egg meat, ginger, garlic, cilantro, chilies, soaked bread,
coriander, cumin, garam masala, salt, lime juice and egg.
Mix thoroughly to blend all ingredients. Do not overblend to avoid grainy texture.
Line a plate with parchment paper. Divide meat mixture into 4 portions and form into patties.
Place the patties on a lined plate and set aside.
Heat about 1/4 cup oil in a large griddle or frying pan over medium heat.
When hot, add the patties (do not overcrowd the pan) and cook on both sides until they register 160 F,
about 5 to 8 minutes on each side.
While the patties are cooking, slice each bun, open and lightly toast on a grill or in a toaster oven.
Butter as desired and spread about 1 tablespoon of mint-coriander chutney on each bun.
Place a cooked patty on each bun and add tomato and onion.
Season with salt if desired, close the bun and serve right away.
Recipe Variations
Instead of the mint-coriander curry, make a simple curry mayonnaise.
Combine 1/2 cup of mayonnaise with 2 teaspoons of lemon juice or lime juice and 2 to 3 teaspoons of curry powder.
Replace the burger buns with flatbread of choice.
How to Store Burgers
Refrigerate Indian-style burgers in a covered container and consume within 4 days.
To freeze the burgers, place them on a baking sheet in the freezer.
When frozen solid, transfer them to resealable freezer bags or vacuum-seal them.
Label with the name and date and store them in the freezer for up to 3 months.
Defrost in the refrigerator for a few hours or overnight.
To reheat the burger patties, Place a rack in a baking pan and heat the oven to 180 fan or 400 F.
Place the burgers on the rack and bake in the preheated oven for about 4 to 6 minutes, or until they are hot.
