Easy chicken traybake
Prep: 15 mins
Cook: 45 mins
Serves 4
Ingredients
8 skin-on chicken thighs
1 x 400g tin chickpeas, drained
2 red onions, cut into wedges
225g/8oz cherry tomatoes
2 tbsp / 30 ml olive oil
1½ tsp smoked paprika
½ lemon, juiced
Method
STEP 1
Heat the oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6.
STEP 2
Toss everything except the lemon in a large roasting tin with plenty of seasoning.
STEP 3
Roast for 40-45 mins until the chicken is golden and cooked through.
STEP 4
Squeeze over the lemon juice and serve.
Nutrition: per serving
kcal 520
fat 30g
saturates 8g
