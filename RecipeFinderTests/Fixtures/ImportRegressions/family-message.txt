Nan's cheese scones
225g self-raising flour
1 tsp baking powder
55g butter, cubed
100g mature cheddar, grated
150ml milk
Rub the butter into the flour and baking powder until it looks like breadcrumbs.
Stir in most of the cheese, then add the milk a bit at a time to make a soft dough.
Pat it out about 2cm thick and cut into rounds.
Top with the rest of the cheese and bake at 220C for 12-15 mins.
