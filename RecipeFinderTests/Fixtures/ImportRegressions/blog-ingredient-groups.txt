Lemon Drizzle Traybake
Prep 15 minutes
Cook 35 minutes
Serves 12
Ingredients
For the sponge:
225g unsalted butter, softened
225g caster sugar
4 large eggs
275g self-raising flour
2 lemons, zested
Drizzle:
1 lemon, juiced
85g caster sugar
To decorate:
2 tbsp lemon curd
Method
1. Heat the oven to 180C and line a 30 x 20cm tin with baking paper.
2. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time.
3. Fold in the flour and lemon zest, spoon into the tin and bake for 35 minutes.
4. Mix the lemon juice and sugar and spoon over the warm sponge.
5. Dot with lemon curd once cool and cut into squares.
