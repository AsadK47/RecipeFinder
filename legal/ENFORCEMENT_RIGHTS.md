❌ **"I didn't mean to violate"** - Intent is often irrelevant  
❌ **"It was an accident"** - Strict liability applies to many claims  
❌ **"I didn't think you'd care"** - Assumption of consent fails  
❌ **"Others are doing it too"** - Not a legal defense  
❌ **"I only used a small part"** - Infringement is not quantitative  
❌ **"It's fair use"** - Very narrow exception, rarely applies  
❌ **"I gave attribution"** - Attribution doesn't equal authorization  
❌ **"I'm not making money"** - Commercial gain not required for liability  
❌ **"I can't afford penalties"** - Financial hardship irrelevant

❌ **"Different jurisdiction"** - International enforcement available  
❌ **"Statute of limitations"** - We file within limitation periods
