## 📅 KEY DEADLINES (FIRST YEAR)

### Critical Dates
- **Corporation Tax Registration:** Within 3 months of starting business
- **First Confirmation Statement:** 5 November 2026
- **First Accounts:** Within 21 months of incorporation (approx. August 2027)
- **First Corporation Tax Return:** 12 months after year-end
- **First Corporation Tax Payment:** 9 months + 1 day after year-end

### 14-Day Notification Requirements
Must notify Companies House within 14 days of:
- Director appointments/resignations
- Registered office address changes
- Share capital changes
- PSC changes
- Company secretary changes (if applicable)

## ⚖️ DIRECTOR DUTIES REMINDER

As director, you must:
1. ✅ Act within powers (follow company constitution)
2. ✅ Promote success of company (act in good faith)
3. ✅ Exercise independent judgment
4. ✅ Exercise reasonable care, skill and diligence
5. ✅ Avoid conflicts of interest
6. ✅ Not accept benefits from third parties
7. ✅ Declare interest in proposed transactions

**Failure to comply can result in:**
- Personal liability
- Criminal prosecution  
- Director disqualification (2-15 years)
- Unlimited fines

## 🚨 COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID

### Don't:
- ❌ Mix personal and business finances
- ❌ Miss filing deadlines
- ❌ Forget to notify changes to Companies House
- ❌ Ignore director duties
- ❌ Trade while insolvent
- ❌ Use company funds for personal use
- ❌ Fail to keep proper records
- ❌ Ignore HMRC deadlines

### Do:
- ✅ Keep detailed financial records
- ✅ Separate business and personal accounts
- ✅ File all documents on time
- ✅ Update Companies House promptly
- ✅ Hold and document board meetings
- ✅ Take professional advice when needed
- ✅ Plan for tax payments
- ✅ Review compliance regularly
