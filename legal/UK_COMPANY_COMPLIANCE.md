## ⚖️ DIRECTOR DUTIES & LIABILITIES

### Statutory Duties (Companies Act 2006, s.170-177)

1. **Act within powers** (s.171)
   - Act in accordance with company's constitution
   - Only exercise powers for purposes given

2. **Promote success of company** (s.172)
   - Act in good faith
   - Promote success for benefit of shareholders
   - Consider long-term consequences
   - Consider employee interests
   - Foster business relationships
   - Consider community and environment
   - Maintain reputation for high standards
   - Act fairly between shareholders

3. **Exercise independent judgment** (s.173)
   - Make own decisions
   - Not blindly follow others

4. **Exercise reasonable care, skill and diligence** (s.174)
   - General knowledge, skill and experience reasonably expected
   - Actual knowledge, skill and experience possessed

5. **Avoid conflicts of interest** (s.175)
   - Avoid situations where interests conflict
   - Disclose conflicts to board

6. **Not accept benefits from third parties** (s.176)
   - No bribes or secret commissions
   - Maintain independence

7. **Declare interest in transactions** (s.177)
   - Disclose nature and extent of interest
   - Disclosure to fellow directors

### Personal Liability Risks

Directors can be personally liable for:
- ❌ Fraudulent trading (s.993)
- ❌ Wrongful trading (Insolvency Act 1986, s.214)
- ❌ Breach of fiduciary duties
- ❌ Disqualification offences
- ❌ Environmental offences
- ❌ Health and safety breaches
- ❌ Late filing penalties (if knowingly allowed)

### Companies House Penalties

**Late Confirmation Statement:**
- Fine up to £5,000
- Company may be struck off

**Late Annual Accounts:**
- Private company filing penalties (automatic):
  - 1 month late: £150
  - 3 months late: £375
  - 6 months late: £750
  - Over 6 months: £1,500

**Failure to Notify Changes:**
- Criminal offense
- Unlimited fines possible
- Director disqualification risk

### HMRC Penalties

**Late Corporation Tax Payment:**
- Interest charged from due date
- Penalties apply for late payment

**Late Corporation Tax Return:**
- £100 penalty if 1 day late
- £200 penalty if 3 months late
- Tax-geared penalties if 6+ months late

### Criminal Offenses

Serious breaches can result in:
- Criminal prosecution
- Unlimited fines
- Imprisonment (up to 2 years for certain offenses)
- Director disqualification (2-15 years)
